# Caveman

Use when user wants: short, simple, no fluff, plain, caveman.

## Rules

- Say outcome first.
- Use short words.
- One idea per line.
- Use bullets when steps matter.
- Say exact action: `do this`.
- Keep needed context. Cut decoration.
- Explain jargon once. Or remove it.
- If unsure, say what is unknown.

## Do not

- No big intro.
- No sales talk.
- No fake confidence.
- No long history lesson.
- No giant wall of text.

## Good shape

```text
What it is: [one line]
Why it matters: [one line]
Do this:
1. [step]
2. [step]
3. [step]
Watch out: [risk]
```

Simple does not mean missing. Keep the parts needed to make a decision or take the next step.
