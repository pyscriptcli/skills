# PRIME Philippines Pitch Deck Brand

Use this reference when the deck is explicitly PRIME-branded or uses PRIME source material. If a current official PRIME brand guide or template is provided, follow it over this quick reference and retain any documented exceptions.

## Voice and visual identity

PRIME's voice is **authoritative, sovereign, premium, and earned, never ostentatious**. Every branded artifact should feel like advice trusted with an institution's built environment. The tagline is **“We Advise. You Advance.”** Use it where it fits; do not force it into every slide.

## Palette

| Color | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| PRIME Blue | `#003366` | Authority field, headings, strong type |
| PRIME Warm White | `#FFFCFB` | Light page/surface, reversed type |
| PRIME Gold | `#C9A84C` | Restrained accent, rule, key distinction; not a large background |
| PRIME Gray | `#181D1E` | Body text and fine details on Warm White |
| PRIME Deep Ink | `#0C0C0E` | Deepest text when Gray lacks weight; never a background |

Use Blue or Warm White for branded backgrounds. Use Gray or Deep Ink only as ink. Avoid pure black and pure white in brand surfaces unless a chart's print rules or the source template require it. Maintain sufficient contrast; Gold is an accent, not a substitute for readable body text.

## Typography

Use only the three PRIME type families and documented fallbacks:

| Job | Typeface | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Display headlines | Cormorant Garamond | Light 300; generous scale and restrained negative tracking |
| Section headings / editorial emphasis | Cormorant Garamond | Light italic or Regular when appropriate |
| Big figures and short impact labels | Bebas Neue | Regular; distinctive, compact display use |
| Body copy | Montserrat | Regular |
| Labels / UI caps | Montserrat | Medium 500, uppercase, tracked |

Fallbacks: Cormorant Garamond → Georgia, serif; Bebas Neue → Impact, sans-serif; Montserrat → Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif. If exact font files exist locally, use and embed them when output permits. Check the export to confirm the intended faces and weights were used. Never claim a fallback is an exact match.

## Logo and geometry

- Use the official PRIME logo asset. Choose the white logo for PRIME Blue and the blue logo for Warm White. Preserve transparency and aspect ratio.
- Never redraw, trace, recolor arbitrarily, or generate the PRIME logo. If an official asset is unavailable, leave a clean space and name the needed asset.
- Favor square, deliberate geometry. Keep card and container corners sharp; curves belong to official brand artwork.
- Keep Gold for restrained accents. Blue is the main authority color; Warm White creates breathing room.

## Charts and data

Use PRIME Blue and PRIME Gold as the core data colors; read the official PRIME data-visualization guide when making charts, maps, dashboards, or regional comparisons. Use the source's units and labels, include a custom legible legend, and do not use Gold to signal a warning.
